  • A showroom environment capable of hosting a live, experiential daylight display

The Partnership

Backed by YuerSky technology and Zenith SkyTech support.

YuerSky develops optical daylight-recreation technology for applications where conventional natural light is unavailable or impractical. Zenith SkyTech brings that technology to the UK and Ireland through a dedicated local partnership, providing customers and showroom partners with a direct point of contact for product, specification, servicing and commercial support.
