Basements & Lower Ground

Daylight that never had anywhere to go.

Below-ground rooms are structurally cut off from daylight — until now, that was permanent.

The Problem

A basement can be beautifully finished and still feel like a basement.

No amount of paint, lighting design or floor-to-ceiling storage changes the fundamental issue: there's no daylight down here, and standard lighting — however well-specified — still reads as artificial. For a space used for hours at a time (a home office, a gym, a media room), that has a real effect on how the room actually feels to spend time in.

The Solution

A daylight-spectrum ceiling void install — no digging required.

The technology installs into the existing ceiling void, with no external structural opening and no excavation. For basements and lower-ground rooms specifically, that's the difference between a viable project and one that never gets past the planning stage.
