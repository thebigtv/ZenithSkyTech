Windowless Interior Rooms

The room in the middle of the plan.

Internal rooms, ensuites and stairwells — spaces the floorplan itself puts out of daylight's reach.

The Problem

Some rooms were never going to get a window, no matter how the plan changed.

An internal bathroom, a stairwell, a windowless study wedged into the middle of a deep floorplan — these aren't oversights, they're the practical result of how a building has to be laid out. Standard lighting makes them usable. It doesn't make them feel like anywhere you'd want to spend time.

The Solution

Genuine daylight spectrum, with no dependency on the floorplan at all.

Because the technology doesn't need a route to the outside — no roof access, no external wall — it applies exactly as well to a windowless ensuite as it does to a full room, regardless of where in the building it sits.
