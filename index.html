The Technology

Real daylight is a spectrum, not a colour. So is this.

YuerSky technology doesn't simulate daylight with a warm bulb — it optically recreates the daylight spectrum itself, shifting through the day the way real sky light does. It's the reason a basement office can feel, genuinely, like it has a window.
